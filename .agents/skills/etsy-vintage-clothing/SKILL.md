# Etsy Vintage Clothing Listing Optimizer

Generates fully optimized Etsy listings for second-hand and vintage clothing, combining
Etsy's standard search algorithm best practices with the specific conventions of vintage
fashion selling. Built from the Etsy Seller Handbook + vintage seller community expertise.

---

## What Makes Vintage Clothing Listings Different

Vintage clothing has unique challenges that general Etsy SEO doesn't cover:

- **Sizing is unreliable** — vintage sizes run 2–4 sizes smaller than modern equivalents;
  buyers need measurements, not just the label size
- **One-of-a-kind inventory** — no quantity variations; every listing is unique
- **Era matters** — buyers search by decade AND by style name (not always the same)
- **Condition is a purchase driver** — must be described honestly and precisely
- **Authenticity signals** — brand labels, made-in tags, fabric composition matter
- **Buyers split into two types**: era-hunters (searching "1970s") AND style-hunters
  (searching "boho maxi dress") — your tags must serve BOTH

---

## Etsy Search Algorithm (Vintage Context)

Etsy search has two phases: **query matching** then **ranking**.

For vintage clothing, query matching depends heavily on tags + title + description because
attributes for clothing (color, size) are less reliable (vintage sizes differ). Your tags
must compensate for what attributes can't accurately convey.

Ranking signals that matter most for vintage:
- **Listing quality score**: first photo CTR + conversion rate (the listing is your one shot)
- **Relevancy**: exact keyword matches in title/tags rank higher — era tags are critical
- **Customer service**: review rating, response rate within 48h
- **Recency**: new listings get a small temporary boost — useful for OOAK (one of a kind) shops
- **Shipping price**: free or low shipping gets priority placement in the Etsy app

> The "Vintage" attribute/category on Etsy requires items to be **at least 20 years old**.
> Items from the early 2000s onward are technically "vintage" as of 2025 — Y2K and early
> 2000s pieces now qualify. Items younger than 20 years should be listed as "second-hand"
> or "pre-loved" in your descriptions, not as vintage.

## Listing Generation Workflow

### STEP 1 — Gather Garment Information

Collect (ask if not provided):

**Identity**
- Type of garment (dress, jacket, blouse, trousers, skirt, coat, etc.)
- Gender presentation (women's, men's, unisex)
- Era / decade (or estimated based on label/style)
- Brand or maker (if label is present)
- Country of origin (from label — "Made in USA," "Made in Italy," etc.)

**Appearance**
- Color(s) — primary and secondary
- Print/pattern (floral, plaid, abstract, solid, geometric, etc.)
- Silhouette/cut (A-line, wrap, straight-leg, oversized, fitted, etc.)
- Fabric/material (cotton, wool, silk, polyester, velvet, denim, etc.)
- Notable details (pockets, buttons, collar type, embroidery, lace trim, etc.)

**Size & Measurements** (critical — see measurement guide below)
- Label size (as-is)
- Modern equivalent size estimate
- Key measurements in both cm and inches

**Condition**
- Overall condition grade (see condition grading system below)
- Specific flaws if any (fading, pilling, small stain, missing button, etc.)

**Vibe / Styling Context**
- Fashion aesthetic (boho, mod, preppy, punk, grunge, minimalist, cottagecore, etc.)
- How it could be styled or worn today
- Occasion it suits (casual, formal, festival, everyday, party, etc.)

---

### STEP 2 — Era + Style Keyword Mapping

This is the most important step for vintage clothing. Map keywords across TWO dimensions:

**Dimension A — Era (how decade-hunters search):**

| Era | Short tags | Full tags | Style name tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1940s | `40s` `1940s` | `forties fashion` | `wartime style` |
| 1950s | `50s` `1950s` | `fifties style` | `rockabilly` `pin up` |
| 1960s | `60s` `1960s` | `sixties vintage` | `mod fashion` `go go` |
| 1970s | `70s` `1970s` | `seventies style` | `boho 70s` `disco` `folk` |
| 1980s | `80s` `1980s` | `eighties fashion` | `power dressing` `new wave` |
| 1990s | `90s` `1990s` | `nineties vintage` | `grunge` `minimalist 90s` |
| 2000s | `y2k` `early 2000s` | `y2k fashion` | `y2k aesthetic` |

> For era tags: always include both the number format ("70s"), the full year ("1970s"),
> AND the decade name ("seventies") when possible — buyers search all three ways.
> But don't fill ALL tags with era variants — balance with style and item-type tags.

**Dimension B — Style-hunters (how aesthetic buyers search):**
These buyers don't care about the decade — they want a specific look:

- Aesthetic tags: `cottagecore`, `dark academia`, `boho chic`, `prairie style`,
  `coastal grandmother`, `balletcore`, `old money`, `quiet luxury`, `maximalist`
- Style descriptors: `flowy maxi dress`, `oversized blazer`, `high waist trousers`,
  `puff sleeve blouse`, `wrap dress`, `a-line skirt`
- Occasion tags: `festival outfit`, `wedding guest`, `date night`, `beach cover`,
  `office outfit`, `vintage bridal`

---

### STEP 3 — Categories & Attributes for Clothing

**Category path** (use most specific available):
- Clothing > Women's Clothing > Dresses > [Maxi / Mini / Midi / Wrap, etc.]
- Clothing > Men's Clothing > Jackets & Coats > [Blazers / Denim, etc.]
- Clothing > Unisex Adult Clothing > ...

**Attributes to always fill:**
- **Color** — add all relevant colors, not just the primary
- **Size** — add the modern equivalent, not the vintage label size
- **Material** — add every fabric present
- **Occasion** — add all that apply (Casual, Formal, Wedding, etc.)
- **Style** — add multiple style attributes
- **Pattern** — if patterned, select every applicable option

> Note: Since vintage sizes run small, set the **Size attribute** to the modern
> equivalent. Explain the discrepancy clearly in the description.

---

### STEP 4 — Write the Title

Formula for vintage clothing titles:

`[Era] [Color] [Fabric/Material] [Garment Type]: [Key Detail or Brand], [Modern Size / Fit]`

Examples:
- `70s Brown Corduroy Wide-Leg Trousers: High Waist, Modern M/L`
- `Vintage 90s Black Velvet Slip Dress: Grunge Minimalist, Size S`
- `1960s Mod Floral A-Line Dress: Cotton, Made in USA, Modern XS/S`
- `Y2K Denim Jacket: Low Rise, Distressed, Unisex Modern M`

Rules:
- Lead with the ERA + color + garment type (what it IS)
- Include the modern size estimate in the title — buyers filter by size
- Keep under ~140 characters, scannable on mobile
- Do NOT keyword-stuff — a buyer should understand the item in 3 seconds
- Brand name in title only if it adds significant value (Levi's, Levi Strauss, Ralph Lauren)

---

### STEP 5 — Write All 13 Tags

Distribute tags across these categories for vintage clothing:

1. **Era — number format** (e.g., `70s dress`, `1970s fashion`)
2. **Era — decade name** (e.g., `seventies style`, `sixties dress`)
3. **Garment + color/material** (e.g., `brown corduroy pants`)
4. **Silhouette/cut** (e.g., `wide leg trousers`, `maxi dress`)
5. **Aesthetic/style name** (e.g., `boho chic`, `dark academia`)
6. **Garment + occasion** (e.g., `vintage party dress`, `festival outfit`)
7. **Recipient / who it's for** (e.g., `gift for her`, `womens vintage`)
8. **Fabric type** (e.g., `velvet dress`, `silk blouse`)
9. **Brand** (if applicable — `levis denim jacket`, `ralph lauren`)
10. **Modern style equivalent** (e.g., `cottagecore dress`, `y2k aesthetic`)
11. **Descriptive long-tail** (e.g., `floral wrap dress`, `high waist jeans`)
12. **Seasonal/occasion** (e.g., `summer vintage dress`, `wedding guest`)
13. **Second type / backup style tag** (diversify — don't repeat slot 5 exactly)

> Tag character limit: 20 characters each. Break longer phrases across two tags.
> Do NOT repeat exact category/attribute phrases. Do NOT use single words.

---

### STEP 6 — Write the Description

Structure for vintage clothing descriptions:

**Para 1 — Keyword lead + era/style hook (2–3 sentences)**
Open with natural-sounding sentences that include your top 2–3 keywords. Set the scene —
what era is it from, what's special about it, what aesthetic does it evoke?

**Para 2 — Garment details (bullet list or short paragraph)**
- Fabric / composition
- Color description (precise — "dusty rose," not just "pink")
- Pattern or print details
- Notable features: pockets, lining, closures, trim, embroidery, etc.
- Brand label / maker's mark / country of origin label (if present)

**Para 3 — Size & measurements (CRITICAL — always include)**
State explicitly:
- Label size (as printed on the garment)
- Modern equivalent size estimate
- Key measurements (provide in both cm and inches):
  - Bust / Chest
  - Waist
  - Hips
  - Length (total garment length from shoulder or waist)
  - Sleeve length (if applicable)
  - Inseam / rise (for trousers)
  - Shoulders (for jackets/tops)

**Para 4 — Condition**
Use the condition grading system (see reference file). Be honest — vintage buyers
accept flaws when they're disclosed. Hidden flaws = bad reviews.

**Para 5 — Styling suggestion (optional but converts well)**
How can this piece fit into a modern wardrobe? Give 1–2 concrete outfit ideas.
Example: "Pair with chunky loafers and a ribbed turtleneck for a perfect 70s-inspired
autumn look."

**Para 6 — Shop note / care instructions**
Mention dry clean / hand wash / spot clean as appropriate. Note if unlined/lined.

---

### STEP 7 — Photos for Vintage Clothing

Photos are the #1 conversion driver for vintage clothing. Recommend:

- [ ] **Hero shot**: flat lay OR on a hanger, clean background, good lighting
- [ ] **On a model/mannequin**: shows drape, fit, proportion — highest converting
- [ ] **Front full-length** (if on model)
- [ ] **Back full-length**
- [ ] **Detail shot**: print, fabric texture, notable feature (lace, buttons, collar)
- [ ] **Label photo**: brand label + care/composition label + country of origin
- [ ] **Condition photo**: any flaws, even minor ones (fading, small stain, wear)
- [ ] **Scale reference**: folded next to a common object OR flat with a measuring tape
- [ ] **Styling inspiration**: worn with modern pieces to show contemporary relevance

> The label photo is unique to vintage and builds enormous buyer trust. Always include it.
> The condition/flaw photo reduces returns and negative reviews dramatically.

---

### STEP 8 — Conversion Tips for Vintage

- **Returns policy**: State clearly. Many vintage sellers do no-returns due to OOAK nature
  — but note this upfront. Missing return policy hurts search ranking.
- **Processing time**: Vintage ships fast — don't over-pad. 1–3 business days is ideal.
- **Shipping**: Free shipping boosts search ranking in the Etsy app. For lightweight
  garments, consider building shipping into the price.
- **Respond quickly**: 48-hour message response is a ranking and Star Seller factor.
- **Price honestly**: Include comparable sales research. Buyers compare listings.
