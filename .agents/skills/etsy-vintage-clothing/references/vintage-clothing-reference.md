# Vintage Clothing Reference — Condition, Eras & Measurements

---

## Condition Grading System

Use these standardized grades in listings. Vintage buyers understand them.

| Grade | Label | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **NWT** | New with Tags | Never worn, original tags still attached |
| **NWoT** | New without Tags | Never worn, tags removed |
| **Excellent** | Excellent Vintage | No visible flaws, light or no wear |
| **Very Good** | Very Good Vintage | Minor signs of wear, no damage |
| **Good** | Good Vintage | Light wear, may have minor flaw (small pilling, faint mark) |
| **Fair** | Fair / As-Is | Noticeable wear or flaw, priced accordingly, clearly disclosed |

**Always describe the specific flaw**, even for Excellent/Very Good:
- "A tiny pinhole at the back hem, not visible when worn"
- "Slight fading at the shoulders consistent with age"
- "One loose button (thread intact, easy fix)"
- "Light pilling on the inner thighs"
- "Very faint underarm mark, barely visible"

Disclosing flaws = fewer returns, better reviews, buyer trust.

## Era Keywords Reference

### Decade → Search Terms

**1930s–40s (Depression & Wartime era)**
- Number: `30s` `40s` `1930s` `1940s`
- Decade name: `thirties` `forties`
- Style names: `wartime fashion` `utility dress` `victory rolls` `swing dress`
- Key silhouettes: padded shoulders, nipped waist, A-line, midi length

**1950s (Post-war / Rock & Roll)**
- Number: `50s` `1950s`
- Decade name: `fifties vintage`
- Style names: `rockabilly` `pin up style` `poodle skirt` `circle skirt`
  `new look fashion` `housewife dress`
- Key silhouettes: full skirts, petticoats, fitted bodice, swing coat

**1960s (Mod / Space Age)**
- Number: `60s` `1960s`
- Decade name: `sixties vintage` `sixties fashion`
- Style names: `mod fashion` `go go style` `space age fashion` `twiggy style`
  `op art` `psychedelic`
- Key silhouettes: mini skirt, shift dress, A-line, geometric prints

**1970s (Boho / Disco)**
- Number: `70s` `1970s`
- Decade name: `seventies style` `seventies fashion`
- Style names: `boho 70s` `disco fashion` `folk style` `hippie` `prairie dress`
  `peasant blouse` `wrap dress 70s` `earth tones`
- Key silhouettes: maxi, wide-leg, bell-bottoms, wrap, peasant

**1980s (Power Dressing / New Wave)**
- Number: `80s` `1980s`
- Decade name: `eighties fashion` `eighties vintage`
- Style names: `power dressing` `new wave fashion` `preppy 80s`
  `aerobics fashion` `big shoulders` `Dynasty style`
- Key silhouettes: power shoulders, oversized blazer, high-waist, bodycon

**1990s (Grunge / Minimalism)**
- Number: `90s` `1990s`
- Decade name: `nineties vintage` `nineties fashion`
- Style names: `grunge fashion` `minimalist 90s` `slip dress 90s`
  `preppy 90s` `90s casual` `heroin chic aesthetic`
- Key silhouettes: slip dress, oversized flannel, crop top, bootcut, plaid

**2000s (Y2K)**
- Era: `y2k` `early 2000s` `2000s fashion`
- Style names: `y2k aesthetic` `y2k fashion` `paris hilton style`
  `low rise y2k` `rhinestone fashion` `butterfly motif`
- Key silhouettes: low-rise, micro mini, cargo pants, velour tracksuit

> Items must be at least 20 years old to qualify as "vintage" on Etsy.
> As of 2025: anything from 2005 or earlier qualifies.
> 2000–2005 era = early Y2K vintage.

## Measurement Guide

### What to Measure and How

Always measure the **garment flat** (not the body). Lay it on a flat surface.

**For Dresses & Tops:**
- **Bust**: measure across the chest at the fullest point, double it
- **Waist**: measure across the narrowest point, double it
- **Hips**: measure across the widest point of the skirt/body, double it
- **Length**: from highest shoulder point (or neckline) to hem
- **Sleeve length**: from shoulder seam to cuff
- **Shoulder width**: across the back, seam to seam

**For Trousers & Skirts:**
- **Waist**: across the waistband, double it
- **Hips**: across the widest point, double it
- **Rise (front/back)**: from crotch seam to waistband top
- **Inseam**: from crotch seam to hem
- **Outseam / Total length**: from waistband top to hem
- **Leg opening**: across the hem, doubled

**For Jackets & Coats:**
- All of the above for tops, plus:
- **Chest**: across fullest point when buttoned, doubled

### Vintage Size Conversion (Approximate)

Vintage sizes run significantly smaller than modern sizing. General guide:

| Vintage label size | Modern US equivalent | Modern EU equiv |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | XS / 0–2 | 32–34 |
| 10 | XS–S / 2–4 | 34–36 |
| 12 | S / 4–6 | 36–38 |
| 14 | S–M / 6–8 | 38–40 |
| 16 | M / 8–10 | 40–42 |
| 18 | M–L / 10–12 | 42–44 |

> This is approximate. **Always provide actual measurements** — never rely on size
> conversion alone. Two buyers with identical measurements can fit differently
> depending on the garment's structure and stretch.

## Common Brand Label Notes

If a brand label is present, always include it — it's a powerful search term:

- **Levi's / Levi Strauss**: Add `levis` and `levis vintage` as tags
- **Ralph Lauren / Polo**: Add `ralph lauren` and `polo vintage`
- **Calvin Klein**: Add `calvin klein` and `ck vintage`
- **Diane von Furstenberg**: Add `dvf` and `wrap dress`
- **Laura Ashley**: Strong vintage association — add `laura ashley` as tag
- **Country Road, etc.**: Add brand name + era if known

**Made in USA / Made in England / Made in Italy labels**: Note in description and tags
when present — adds provenance appeal and helps vintage authenticity signals.

## Tips Specific to One-of-a-Kind (OOAK) Listings

Since every vintage piece is unique, Etsy's recency boost (small ranking bump on new
listings) matters more for vintage shops than for shops with repeatable inventory.

Strategies:
- Add new listings regularly to keep getting recency boosts
- Use Shop Stats to find which search terms bring visitors — adjust tags on similar pieces
- Diversify your tag palette across listings so your shop matches a wide range of queries
- Use your About section to reinforce the "class" of product you sell (e.g., "70s vintage
  clothing") — this helps Etsy associate your shop with those search terms

**Renewing listings just for the recency boost is not effective** — the boost is tiny and
short-lived. Focus on quality listings and fresh inventory instead.
