# Etsy Search Deep Dive — Advanced Reference

Source: Etsy Seller Handbook (August 2025). Read this file when you need more detail
on the ranking algorithm, troubleshooting visibility, or advanced optimization strategies.

---

## Ranking Factors In Detail

### 1. Relevancy
Etsy looks holistically at: title, tags, attributes, descriptions, categories, first photo,
and reviews. Exact keyword matches rank higher than partial matches. This is why diverse,
specific tags matter more than repeating the same keyword across multiple tags.

### 2. Listing Quality Score
- Starts neutral for every new listing
- Built from: clicks, favorites, add-to-carts, purchases
- New listings get a **small temporary boost** (hours to days) so Etsy can gather data
- For vintage/one-of-a-kind shops, Etsy uses historical shop popularity to predict future
  listing performance
- After vacation mode: quality scores stay frozen while competitors accumulate fresh data —
  expect a dip in ranking when you return; it recovers as buyers interact with your listings

### 3. Listing Quality — Photo Impact
The first photo is what appears in search results. It directly drives CTR (click-through
rate). A high CTR feeds the quality score. A bad first photo = fewer clicks = lower score.

### 4. Shipping Price
- Lower shipping = better ranking
- Free shipping to US buyers = priority placement in the Etsy app
- US free shipping guarantee = prioritized in email/social/TV advertising too
- Consider absorbing some shipping cost into item price to offer lower displayed shipping

### 5. Customer Service Score (last 3 months)
Four factors:
- **Average review rating** — aim for 4–5 stars
- **Message response rate** — respond to initial messages within 48 hours
  (auto-responders count; "From Etsy" inbox messages excluded)
- **On-time shipping with tracking**
- **Case rate** — only cases under $250 where refund was taken from Payment account;
  cases covered by Etsy Purchase Protection don't count against you

### 6. Context Specific Ranking (CSR)
Etsy personalizes results per shopper based on their browsing/purchase history across
the platform. Two buyers searching the same phrase may see different results. This is
why you cannot reliably track your rank by searching yourself.

### 7. Translations
- Tags/titles should be in your shop's configured language
- Etsy machine-translates for international shoppers
- Self-added translations outperform machine translations — worth doing for key markets

## Advanced Tag Strategy

### The 20-Character Limit Workaround
If your ideal phrase is >20 characters, break it across multiple complementary tags:
- "personalized wedding gift" (25 chars) → `personalized gift` + `wedding gift`
- "minimalist diamond engagement ring" → `minimalist jewelry` + `diamond ring` + `engagement ring`

### Avoid These Common Tag Mistakes
| Mistake | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| "ring", "bracelet", "necklace" as separate tags | Single words are too broad, low conversion |
| Tag = exact category phrase | Wastes a slot — category already acts as a tag |
| Tag = exact attribute phrase | Same — attribute already acts as a tag |
| Keyword stuffing titles | Reduces CTR, lowers quality score |
| Misspellings in tags | Etsy already handles common typos automatically |
| Same keyword repeated in 3+ tags | Wastes 2 slots that could reach new audiences |

### Regional Phrases Worth Adding
Etsy handles common spelling variants (jewellery/jewelry) automatically, but does NOT
handle regional vocabulary. Examples where adding a tag helps:
- "thong sandals" for Australian buyers searching what US sellers call "flip flops"
- "jumper" vs "sweater" for UK audiences
- "serviette" vs "napkin"

## Shop-Level Optimization (affects ranking)

### Shop Quality Signals
- **Shop icon** — required; represents your brand in search results
- **Shop banner** — builds brand trust
- **About section** — Etsy confirmed it positively affects search ranking. Write a
  genuine story about your process and why you make what you make.
- **Shop policies** — complete policies (returns, exchanges, shipping) build buyer
  confidence

### Star Seller Badge
Does NOT change ranking position, but:
- Shows as a trust badge in search results
- Buyers can filter search results to show only Star Sellers
- Higher conversion = better quality score = indirect ranking benefit
Requirements (checked monthly on the 1st):
- 95%+ message response rate within 24h
- 95%+ on-time dispatch with tracking
- 5+ orders and $300+ in sales in the past 3 months
- Average 4.8+ star rating

## Using Shop Stats to Improve Listings

Instead of searching for your own listings (unreliable due to CSR), use Shop Stats:
- **Traffic details page**: see most-visited listings + the search terms driving traffic
- Find **underperforming listings** (low views): compare their tags to top performers
- Could you transplant successful tags from high-traffic listings to low-traffic ones?
- Identify which search queries are already bringing buyers → reinforce those keywords

## Description SEO (Since 2022)

Etsy now reads listing descriptions for both:
1. **Query matching** (on Etsy search)
2. **External indexing** (Google and other search engines)

Best practices:
- Lead with 1–2 sentences containing your most important keywords naturally
- Do NOT copy-paste your title — craft different, natural-sounding sentences
- Structure: keywords first → product details → brand story
- Short and informative > long and padded

## Etsy Ads & Search Ranking

Running Etsy Ads does NOT influence organic search ranking. Ads occupy designated
advertising slots in search results — separate from organic results. The two systems
are independent. Ads are useful for boosting visibility while building organic quality
score on new listings.

## AI-Powered Tools (2025)

Etsy has introduced optional AI tools:
- **Title suggestions**: available in Search Visibility dashboard for eligible English
  listings — suggests clearer, buyer-friendly titles based on current title, first photo,
  and description
- **Attribute suggestions**: Search Visibility dashboard surfaces missing attributes
  that could improve discoverability
- These are optional and supplement, not replace, manual optimization

## Checklist: Etsy Search Optimization (Official 10-Point List)

From the Etsy Seller Handbook Chapter 7:
1. Use all 13 tags with varied, multi-word phrases
2. Choose the most specific subcategory available
3. Fill in all relevant attributes
4. Write clear, descriptive titles that lead with what the item is
5. Add keywords naturally to the first sentences of your description
6. Use high-quality photos (especially the first/hero image)
7. Include a return policy on every listing
8. Set accurate processing times
9. Offer competitive shipping prices (free shipping if viable)
10. Maintain excellent customer service (reviews, response time, on-time shipping)
